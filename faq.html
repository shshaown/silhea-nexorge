A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Thoughtful answers.

Is SILHÉA a real clinic?+

No. It is a fictional NEXORGE portfolio concept demonstrating an aesthetic-clinic website and product design.

Can I buy these products?+

No. REFINE, DEW and VEIL are packaging concepts, not tested formulations or products for sale.

Are the women shown real patients?+

No. All portraits are original AI-generated CGI imagery. They do not show treatment outcomes.

Can I book an appointment?+

The consultation page prepares a local brief for demonstration. It does not submit an enquiry or book an appointment.

How do I choose real post-procedure care?+

Ask the qualified clinician responsible for your treatment. Product suitability and timing depend on your skin, procedure and healing.

Does scar care mean treating an open wound?+

No. Silicone scar gels and sheets are for fully healed skin. Your treating clinician should guide wound care and when scar care can begin.
