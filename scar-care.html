The care
in between.

A brief introduction to silicone scar care after skin has healed.

A scar is part of the skin’s response to a healed wound. Its appearance can continue to change over time, and the right care depends on the person and the procedure.

Why silicone?

Silicone gels and sheets are used in scar management. They help maintain moisture around a healed scar. This is a product category, not a guarantee about the fictional REFINE concept.

Timing matters.

Silicone scar products belong on fully healed skin, not open wounds. Your treating clinician can advise whether they are appropriate and when to begin. Follow their plan and the instructions for the real product.

A useful conversation.

  • Is my skin fully healed?
  • Is silicone appropriate for this scar?
  • Which product and instructions should I follow?
  • When should I return for review?

General education, not individual medical advice.
